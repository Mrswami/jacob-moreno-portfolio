# UX & Human Interface Viability Strategist Skill

## Mission
Analyze the site from a human user's perspective to guarantee maximum visual engagement, intuitive navigation, low cognitive load, and accessible interactions.

## Responsibilities
1. **User Experience Audit**: Evaluate information architecture, visual weight, font legibility, and contrast ratios.
2. **Touch & Ergonomics**: Ensure call-to-action buttons, admin access triggers, and navigation targets are easily discoverable and comfortable on all screens.
3. **Human Diagnostics**: Identify points of friction, confusing UI states, or misleading micro-copy.

## Workflow Checklist
- Review touch target sizing and spacing.
- Inspect micro-copy for clarity, tone, and system feedback.
- Confirm visual hierarchy guides user attention seamlessly from hero to projects/experience.
