======================================================================
ATXLETSPLAY - PROJECT PURPOSE
======================================================================

atxLetsPlay is an interactive community infrastructure platform engineered specifically for Austin-based live events, gaming meetups, and local civic/interactive gatherings.

Primary Goals:
1. Provide a central real-time hub for local event discovery and live event participation.
2. Enable instantaneous group sync for multi-participant activities in Austin.
3. Foster local developer & gamer community connections with accessible, low-latency interfaces.
